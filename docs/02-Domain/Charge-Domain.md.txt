# Charge Domain

## Purpose

The Charge domain is responsible for defining charge calculation rules, generating charges for Units, and tracking the financial status of those charges.

---

# Charge Plan

Represents a collection of rules used to calculate charges for a specific period.

## Examples

- Monthly Residential Plan
- Commercial Building Plan
- Seasonal Maintenance Plan

## Responsibilities

- Define how charges are calculated.
- Define charge components.
- Define the billing frequency.
- Define the effective date of the plan.
- Provide rules for calculating Unit charges.

---

# Charge Component

Represents one component of a Charge calculation.

A Charge Plan may contain multiple Charge Components.

## Component Types

- Fixed
- Area Based
- Resident Count Based
- Parking Based
- Custom

---
