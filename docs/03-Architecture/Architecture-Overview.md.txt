# BuildingHub Architecture Overview

## Architecture Style

BuildingHub uses a Modular Monolith architecture combined with Clean Architecture principles.

The system is initially deployed as a single application but is internally divided into independent business modules.

---

# Why Modular Monolith?

BuildingHub is designed as a commercial product.

A Modular Monolith provides:

- Simple deployment
- Lower infrastructure cost
- Faster development
- Easier debugging
- Clear business boundaries
- Better separation than a traditional monolith
- Possibility to extract modules into microservices later
