# Resident Domain

## Purpose

The Resident domain is responsible for managing people, their relationships with units, ownership, tenancy, residency, family members, and vehicles.

---

# Person

Represents a real individual who interacts with the system.

A Person may have different relationships with one or more Units over time.

## Examples

- Owner
- Tenant
- Resident
- Family Member

## Properties

- First Name
- Last Name
- National ID
- Phone Number
- Email
- Date of Birth
- Status

---

# Unit Person Relationship

Represents the relationship between a Person and a Unit.

## Relationship Types

- Tenant
- Resident
- Family Member

## Properties

- Person
- Unit
- Relationship Type
- Start Date
- End Date
- Is Primary

## Rules

1. A Person may have relationships with multiple Units.
2. A Unit may have multiple related Persons.
3. Relationships may change over time.
4. Historical relationships must be preserved.
5. An inactive relationship must not be deleted if it is part of historical records.

---

# Unit Ownership

Represents the ownership relationship between a Person and a Unit.

A Unit may have multiple owners.

Ownership may change over time.

## Properties

- Person
- Unit
- Ownership Percentage
- Start Date
- End Date
- Is Primary Owner

## Business Rules

1. A Unit may have multiple owners.
2. Ownership percentage must be greater than zero.
3. Active ownership percentages should total 100%.
4. Ownership history must be preserved.
5. Previous ownership records must not be physically deleted.
6. A Person may own multiple Units.
7. A Unit may have multiple owners.

---

# Vehicle

Represents a vehicle owned or used by a Person.

## Vehicle Types

- Car
- Motorcycle
- Van
- Other

## Properties

- Person
- Vehicle Type
- Brand
- Model
- Color
- License Plate
- Description

## Rules

1. A Person may have multiple Vehicles.
2. A Vehicle belongs to one Person.
3. Vehicle information may be updated over time.
4. Historical vehicle records should be preserved when required.

---

# Parking Assignment

Represents the relationship between a Vehicle and a Parking Unit.

A parking assignment may change over time.

## Properties

- Vehicle
- Parking Unit
- Start Date
- End Date

## Business Rules

1. A Vehicle may be assigned to a Parking Unit.
2. A Parking Unit may be assigned to a Vehicle.
3. A Vehicle may change its assigned Parking Unit over time.
4. Historical parking assignments must be preserved.
5. A Parking Unit should not be assigned to multiple active Vehicles unless explicitly allowed by the Community rules.
