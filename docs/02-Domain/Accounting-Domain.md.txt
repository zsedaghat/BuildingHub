# Accounting Domain

## Purpose

The Accounting domain is responsible for managing the financial records of a Community.

It records financial transactions using a double-entry accounting model.

The Accounting domain supports:

- Income
- Expenses
- Receivables
- Payables
- Payments
- Refunds
- Account balances
- Financial reporting
- Audit history

---

# Accounting Principles

The Accounting domain follows the double-entry accounting principle.

Every financial transaction must have at least:

- One Debit entry
- One Credit entry

The total Debit amount must equal the total Credit amount.
