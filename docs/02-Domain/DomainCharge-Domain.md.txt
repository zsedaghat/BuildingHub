# Charge Domain

## Purpose

The Charge domain is responsible for defining charge calculation rules, generating charges for units, and tracking the financial status of those charges.

---

## Charge Plan

Represents a collection of rules used to calculate charges for a specific period.

### Examples

- Monthly Residential Plan
- Commercial Building Plan
- Seasonal Maintenance Plan

---

## Charge Component

Represents one component of a charge calculation.

### Component Types

- Fixed
- Area Based
- Resident Count Based
- Parking Based
- Custom

---

## Charge

Represents the calculated amount owed by a Unit for a specific billing period.

### Properties

- Unit
- Charge Plan
- Billing Period
- Amount
- Due Date
- Status

### Statuses

- Draft
- Issued
- Partially Paid
- Paid
- Overdue
- Cancelled

---

## Charge Rules

1. A Charge belongs to exactly one Unit.
2. A Charge belongs to one billing period.
3. A Charge is calculated based on a Charge Plan.
4. A Charge may contain multiple Charge Components.
5. A Charge must preserve the calculated amount after issuance.
6. Changes to a Charge Plan must not modify previously issued Charges.
7. A Charge may be paid by one or more Persons.
