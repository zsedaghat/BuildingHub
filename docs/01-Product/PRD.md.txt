# Product Vision

BuildingHub is a SaaS-ready Building & Community Management ERP designed for residential complexes, commercial buildings, and property management companies.

The goal is to provide a scalable, secure, and modern platform for managing buildings, residents, accounting, payments, maintenance, and communication.

---

# Objectives

- Building Management
- Resident Management
- Charge Management
- Accounting
- Payment Management
- Reports
- Notifications
- Multi Tenant
- SaaS Ready
