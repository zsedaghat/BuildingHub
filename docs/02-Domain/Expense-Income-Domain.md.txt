# Expense and Income Domain

## Purpose

The Expense and Income domain is responsible for managing operational financial activities that occur in a Community.

It provides business-level operations for recording:

- Expenses
- Supplier invoices
- Other income
- Refunds
- Operational payments

The Accounting domain is responsible for recording the final financial transactions.

---

# Separation of Responsibilities

The Expense and Income domain represents business operations.

The Accounting domain represents financial accounting records.

```text
Business Operation
        ↓
Expense / Income
        ↓
Accounting Transaction
